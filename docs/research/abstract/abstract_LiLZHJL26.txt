The realm of expensive optimization applications poses significant challenges due to the high computational costs associated with achieving optimal solutions. Multi-fidelity optimization has emerged as a pivotal approach to mitigate these costs, leveraging the synergy between low-fidelity and high-fidelity problems to enhance optimization efficiency. However, existing methods have limitations in their expressiveness for accurately modeling the complex relationships between fidelity levels, potentially resulting in compromised optimization performance. To addresses these limitations, we introduce a multi-fidelity optimization method that used nonlinear autoregressive Gaussian process regression with composite kernels to better represent the intricate relationships within the data. This allows for more accurate predictions. Furthermore, we introduce a Pareto-based multi-objective sampling strategy for improving model accuracy, where exploration and exploitation are treated as separate objectives. This formulation enables the selection of diverse candidate points from the Pareto set and is further integrated with multiple fidelity management strategies, allowing effective coordination between sampling decisions and fidelity selection. The proposed method is validated on two synthetic benchmark suites and 15 multi-fidelity hyperparameter optimization (HPO) tasks, encompassing 130 test cases in total. Extensive experiments against five state-of-the-art algorithms demonstrate that the proposed method consistently achieves superior performance, finding better solutions in 106 out of 130 cases while reducing computational cost by up to 50%.
