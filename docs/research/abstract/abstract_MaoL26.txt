Many real-world optimization problems are non-stationary, where both objective functions and constraints evolve over time due to changing environments. Existing Constrained Bayesian Optimization (CBO) methods are primarily designed for static landscapes and fail to adapt to such temporal shifts. This paper introduces DCBO, a CBO framework for dynamic problems under limited evaluation budgets, which leverages historical information to track evolving constraint boundaries while balancing exploitation within the feasible region. We introduce an ensemble model that aggregates historical models from previous time steps to predict the objective and constraint functions at the next time step. To navigate the search space, we propose an adaptive acquisition function that dynamically balances exploitation within the feasible region and exploration along the constraint boundary. Extensive experiments and ablation studies on synthetic benchmarks and real-world applications across various domains demonstrate the efficacy of our proposed method. The source code is publicly available at https://anonymous.4open.science/r/DCBO-D0FC/README.md.
